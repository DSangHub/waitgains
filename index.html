Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How much can I earn?
You receive 60% of ad revenue per play. With 100 calls per day, you could earn approximately $60/day in ad revenue at standard CPM rates—that's over $1,500/month.
Does this cost me anything?
Setup is completely free. We only take a percentage of ad revenue—you pay nothing unless you're earning. The Professional plan ($99/mo) adds premium features.
What phone systems does this work with?
All of them. We integrate with traditional phone lines, VoIP, mobile networks, RingCentral, Microsoft Teams Phone, Cisco, Avaya, and most major PBX systems.
Can my callers skip the ads?
No—ads play during hold time, which would otherwise be silence or generic music. Callers earn a 10% revenue share for listening,
